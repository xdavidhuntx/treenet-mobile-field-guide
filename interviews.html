FIELD DIRECTION

A-ROLL SCRIPTS THAT DON’T RAMBLE

Use each card as a direction prompt—not a speech to memorize. One answer should create tension, deliver one useful fact and stop after the payoff.

8–15 SEC ANSWERS

Before rolling

Give the speaker a narrow target before asking the question.

TRIGGER WORDS ARE NOT A CHECKLIST.

Choose one or two that fit the person’s real answer. Do not force every phrase into one soundbite.

Founder prompts

The founder owns the company-level story: scale, ambition, vision, team and emotional payoff.

FOUNDER VOICEWHY THIS BUILD MATTERS

Company-level context. Approved claim language. One decisive point per answer.

Installer prompts

Installers own the firsthand story: what their hands are doing, what is difficult and what people never see.

INSTALLER VOICEHOW THE WORK ACTUALLY HAPPENS

Direct experience. Specific craft. No company-wide or engineering claims outside their role.
