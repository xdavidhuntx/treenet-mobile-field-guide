Exact capture handoff

Build two clean source clips whose centers can be joined at peak blur.

CLIP A · IPHONE

HUMAN HOOK

  • Use the rear 1× iPhone camera in vertical 4K—not selfie view.
  • Use 60fps in strong daylight for retiming flexibility; 30fps is sufficient when light is limited.
  • Lock exposure and focus on the founder. Keep his face and torso on the vertical centerline.
  • Record a full-body or knees-up master with generous environment; begin the edit digitally cropped rather than shooting too tight.
  • Keep the phone reasonably still. Natural phone texture is useful; uncontrolled wobble is not.
  • Record three takes with three seconds of clean pre-roll and post-roll.
  • Use clean DJI Mic audio or a separate clean narration take that can continue over the aerial.
  • Founder stands on solid ground or an installer-approved rigid position. No tripod goes on the TreeNet.
CLIP B · DJI AIR 2S

PRIVACY-TIGHT SCALE

  • The Air 2S does not shoot native vertical; preserve enough width in the highest-resolution approved mode for a 9:16 crop.
  • Capture one smooth 8–12 second pullback and one steadier safety plate.
  • Center the TreeNet—not the estate. Use a steep oblique or top-down view with immediate canopy dominant.
  • Ascend and pull backward smoothly with a fixed gimbal angle. Avoid aggressive yaw or orbiting.
  • Capture a clean plate before adding participants when practical.
  • Set a privacy-approved maximum altitude and framing. A broader estate reveal is not permission to make the installation feel bigger.
  • Pilot judgment, people clearance, weather and site permission override the planned shot.
NO TRIPOD ON THE TREENET.

If the operator stands on the bouncing net, use a compact handheld setup, short takes, deliberate starts/stops, crop margin and a steadier safety take. Catalyst is a finishing option—not permission for careless capture.

Resolve + Fusion recipe

Make the splice when neither source is readable, then let the real aerial settle into proof.

01

Timeline

Use a 1080×1920 vertical timeline. A 30fps base is the simplest match for a 5.4K/30 Air 2S plate.

02

Phone crop

Begin the wide iPhone source around 2.0–2.3× and animate to 1.0× over roughly 12–16 frames.

03

Acceleration

Use a strong accelerating curve; do not make the pullback linear.

04

Blur

Build radial/zoom blur around the founder and peak it at the splice. Add motion blur; avoid Smooth Cut.

05

Hidden cut

Cut at maximum blur. If needed, add only a 1–2 frame exposure bloom.

06

Drone crop

Start the drone plate approximately 3–5× cropped around the same visual center, depending on source resolution.

07

Drone ramp

Animate to 1.0× over roughly 18–28 frames while ramping from about 300–400% speed down to 100%.

08

Resolve blur

Keyframe blur from maximum to zero in roughly 8–12 frames; then let the physical drone move continue.

09

Perimeter

Stabilize first if needed. Use a Fusion Paint polyline or dotted SVG and Write On over roughly 12–18 frames.

10

Statistics

Area first, rope second, one number at a time. Each figure locks only after founder approval.

TreeNet sound design

Narration leads. Sound supports the reveal without turning it into a trailer.

FRAME ONE

Clean founder voice starts immediately—no music-only pre-roll.

PULLBACK

One short whoosh through the radial zoom.

AERIAL READS

A restrained low-impact hit when the net becomes legible.

OUTLINE

Soft ticking or zip texture while the dotted perimeter writes.

STAT LOCKS

Small tonal blip for each founder-approved number.

MUSIC

Keep the bed roughly 6–10dB below the voice during the hook; avoid an oversized cinematic boom.

Founder review gate

No bracketed field becomes a public factual claim until this review is complete.

Adopt / avoid

Keep the escalation and proof discipline. Reject anything that weakens truth, privacy or safety.

ADOPT
  • Start the reveal before the verbal sentence ends.
  • Use the founder as the human scale anchor.
  • Hide the camera mismatch at maximum blur.
  • Layer proof: object → outline → area → rope → people.
  • Cut literally on decisive words.
  • Return to people quickly after the aerial.
  • Use a compatible final frame to make the loop feel intentional.
AVOID
  • Holding on the founder for several seconds before the reveal.
  • Trying to fly a physically continuous move from the founder’s face.
  • Using a broad aerial that maps the private property.
  • Drawing a perimeter around an unverified footprint.
  • Publishing rope totals that mix purchased, waste and installed material.
  • Calling the hook an official, certified or Guinness world record.
  • Using Smooth Cut across foliage and a face.
  • Tracking graphics over a fast yawing or orbiting plate.
