HDR decision rule

Movement determines the capture method.

STATIC + TRIPOD5-FRAME HDR

Only for an empty, still installation with extreme canopy-to-shadow contrast. Add one single RAW safety.

SLIGHT LEAF MOVEMENT3-FRAME HDR

Reduce the stack and inspect for ghosting. Use the center RAW if the merge fails.

PEOPLE / WEAVING / WINDSINGLE RAW

Protect shutter speed, expression and sharpness. Never bracket action.

DRONE HOVER5-FRAME 0.7 EV AEB

Static aerial only. Add a single DNG because aircraft drift and leaves can ghost.

FILTER NOTE

The PolarPro Helix 2–5 stop filter is a VND—not a circular polarizer. Remove it for normal stills and bracket stacks unless you deliberately need a slower shutter.

Sony A7 III presets

Built for the 16–35mm GM, 24–70mm GM, tripod and no flash.

SONY 01 · STATIC HDR HERO

Empty installation, clean wides and static architectural frames

  1. File format: RAW + JPEG; RAW File Type: Uncompressed; JPEG Fine; 3:2 full frame.
  2. Exposure: Manual · ISO 100 · f/8 · set the center shutter from the histogram without clipping wet highlights or sky.
  3. Drive: Cont. Bracket. Default 5 frames at 2.0 EV for a truly static extreme-contrast scene; use 3 frames at 2.0 EV when foliage moves.
  4. Bracket Settings: 2-sec self-timer; bracket order - → 0 → + for tidy stacks.
  5. Focus: AF-S + Flexible Spot; focus about one-third into the scene, verify magnified focus, then switch to MF for the full stack.
  6. Stabilization: SteadyShot OFF on the locked tripod; electronic front-curtain ON; Silent Shooting OFF.
  7. Lens: 16–35mm around 18–24mm for hero scale; avoid forcing 16mm if edge stretch distorts the net.
  8. White balance: fixed custom/Kelvin for the sequence; do not let AWB change between bracket frames.
  9. VND: OFF. Remove the 2–5 stop VND unless a deliberate long exposure is required.
  10. Always capture a separate correctly exposed single RAW safety frame after the bracket.
SONY 02 · PEOPLE + ACTIVE CRAFT

Installers, weaving, reactions, crossings and moving foliage

  1. File format: RAW + JPEG; RAW File Type: Uncompressed; JPEG Fine; no HDR bracket.
  2. Exposure: Manual with Auto ISO 100–3200; protect highlights and keep exposure consistent through each action.
  3. Shutter: minimum 1/250 for people; 1/500 or faster for weaving hands and energetic movement.
  4. Aperture: f/2.8–f/4 for one person or details; f/4–f/5.6 for small groups and environmental context.
  5. Focus: AF-C + Eye AF for people; Zone or Flexible Spot for hands, rope and craft details.
  6. Drive: single frame for deliberate portraits; short Low/Mid bursts for unrepeatable movement.
  7. Stabilization: SteadyShot ON handheld; keep the VND off unless intentionally slowing shutter.
  8. Lens: 24–70mm for people/craft; 16–35mm only when spatial context materially improves the image.
  9. White balance: lock one custom/Kelvin value for each sequence; RAW remains adjustable later.

DJI Air 2S presets

Official specification: 20 MP, fixed f/2.8 and 3/5-frame AEB at fixed 0.7 EV spacing.

AIR 2S 01 · STATIC AERIAL HDR

Tightly bounded, static scale frames only

  1. Photo format: JPEG + DNG (RAW) · 20 MP · 3:2 · no digital zoom.
  2. ISO 100. The Air 2S aperture is fixed at f/2.8; exposure control comes from shutter and ISO.
  3. AEB: 5 bracketed frames at the camera’s fixed 0.7 EV spacing.
  4. White balance: fixed Daylight/Cloudy/custom value for the full aerial sequence.
  5. Hover, settle, confirm level horizon and frame edges, then capture the bracket without moving the aircraft.
  6. Capture a separate single DNG safety exposure beside every bracket.
  7. Review for leaf movement, aircraft drift and ghosting; if present, deliver the best single DNG instead of forcing an HDR merge.
AIR 2S 02 · PEOPLE, WIND + MOVEMENT

Human scale or any scene likely to ghost

  1. Photo format: JPEG + DNG (RAW) · 20 MP · 3:2 · Single Shot.
  2. ISO 100 whenever possible; raise only if needed to preserve shutter speed.
  3. Aim for 1/500 or faster when people, leaves or the net are moving.
  4. Use fixed white balance and no digital zoom.
  5. Shoot two or three deliberate single frames after the aircraft settles—do not machine-gun an unsafe hover.
  6. Keep the frame bounded to the net, immediate trunks and canopy; no estate-mapping wide.
