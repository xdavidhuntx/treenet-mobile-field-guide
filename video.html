Camera bible

These global settings govern the mapped recipes below. Every exposure remains a tested starting framework—not a blind command.

01

Primary capture

FX3 internal 4K 10-bit 4:2:2, S-Log3 / S-Gamut3.Cine, Cine EI. Base 800 is the daylight/canopy starting point; move to Base 12,800 only when the underside genuinely requires it—not as a convenience.

02

Motion cadence

23.98p / 1⁄50 for natural-time story, dialogue, walkthroughs and hero holds. 59.94p / 1⁄125 for running, feet, hands, bounce and reactions. 119.88p / 1⁄250 only for a few emphasis inserts.

03

Exposure method

Use ND to preserve shutter and chosen aperture. In S-Log3, place an 18% gray card near 41 IRE and typical skin roughly 52–58 IRE, then protect sky/net highlights with waveform and zebras.

04

White balance

Set a custom white balance in each major lighting zone and lock it. The green net and canopy can contaminate skin; Auto WB will make matching harder.

05

Vertical composition

Mount the FX3 natively 9:16 for hero takes. Keep faces, hands and the key connection out of UI-heavy top/bottom edges. Drone footage must hold the subject inside a center-safe 9:16 crop.

06

Rig choice

Use the light internal-record build on the net. Reserve the Ninja V and tripod for interviews or solid-ground locked shots. Stabilization never replaces safe footing or a spotter.

07

Audio

Record dialogue and sound plates at 48 kHz / 24-bit. Monitor with headphones. Capture a 23.98p natural-sound safety whenever the main picture take is slow motion.

08

Status of settings

Every recipe is a tested starting framework—not a blind exposure command. Recheck light, histogram/waveform, focus, stabilization crop and privacy edges at the real position.
