MANDATORY

SAFETY, PRIVACY + MEDIA

Creative urgency, the delivery deadline and a short weather window never override installer judgment, pilot judgment or the client’s private-site requirements.

NO EXCEPTIONS

Go / no-go

Weather guidance never overrides current site conditions.

Privacy perimeter

The property is not the story. Barbados may be named; the estate and precise location may not.

NEVER CAPTURE OR PUBLISH
  • Private property owner without separate explicit authorization
  • Estate name, gate, address, signage or security systems
  • Road-to-installation journey or access route
  • Recognizable residences, outbuildings or neighboring parcels
  • Coastline relationships or distant landmarks that triangulate the site
  • Broad drone reveals exposing estate scale, boundaries or layout
  • Precise geotags, coordinates or map screenshots
  • Identifiable staff, faces, vehicles or license plates without approval
SAFE ESTABLISHING LANGUAGE
  • Canopy moving in wind
  • Macro leaves, bark, rope and net texture
  • Sky framed through approved trees
  • Tight foliage reveal into the installation
  • Wides whose edges remain inside the installation zone
  • Drone compositions tightly cropped to net, trunks and immediate canopy

Media protection

The deadline is not permission to destroy or compress the source archive.

Claim handling

Use the proven marketing hook without personally certifying an unsupported record.
